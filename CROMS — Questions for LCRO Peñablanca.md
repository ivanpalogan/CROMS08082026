# CROMS — Questions for LCRO Peñablanca

One list, for the office. Pulled from §14 of `CROMS — Agency Requirements Backlog.md`
(Phase 2, item 5). Answered items are already struck through there and are not repeated
here. Nothing below has been guessed at or built — each answer unblocks a specific,
already-scoped piece of work.

---

## A. Marriage

1. **Another-province attachment** — which document proves a licence was obtained in
   another province for a wedding solemnized here? (The trigger itself is confirmed:
   licence-from-elsewhere, wedding-here. CROMS already has a working attachment slot
   for this — it just doesn't name a required document yet, pending this answer.)
2. **Marriage registration** — after the office receives the Certificate of Marriage back,
   how is the registration itself recorded? Which registry number, dates, signatures or
   other details do staff add at that point (as opposed to at the application stage)?
3. **CROMS ↔ PhilCRIS** — where does CROMS's job end and PhilCRIS's begin, for marriage?
4. **Counselling** — is a counselling certificate required across the whole 18–25 age
   band in practice? Is CENOMAR required?
5. **RA 10354 §15** — is a family-planning certificate enforced locally for marriage
   applicants?
6. **Degree of Relationship of Contracting Parties** — Municipal Form 90 asks for this
   (Family Code Arts. 37–38). CROMS has no field for it today. Should it be captured?
   Does anything act on the answer (e.g. refuse a licence), or is it record-only?
7. **Parental Advice — deceased parent** — the form marks a non-signing parent
   "(DECEASED)" by hand. Should CROMS record *why* a parent did not sign (deceased /
   absent / refused), or does the office prefer to keep writing that by hand on the
   printed form?

## B. Death registration

8. What exactly do staff enter when accepting/registering a Death Certificate?
9. Does the office need CROMS to do more than store the document and record basic
   registration data for a death?
10. What is the relationship between CROMS and the death-registration system the office
    already uses?

## C. Case tracking (Legitimation / Supplemental Report / Legal Instruments / Court Order)

11. What are the office's real internal stages for each of these four? CROMS currently
    tracks them generically as Filed → Under Review → Decision → PSA Endorsement — an
    assumption built from statute (RA 9858, RA 9255, Rule 108, and PSA's own
    supplemental-report rule), not from the office's own practice. Is that the right
    shape, or does the office run something different?

## D. BREQS (PSA copy requests)

12. What is the office's accepted valid-ID list for a BREQS request?
13. Is CENOMAR ever requested through BREQS, or only birth/marriage/death copies?

## E. Fees

14. What are the **burial permit** and **transfer-of-cadaver** amounts? (Blank on the
    fee card the office already supplied.)
15. Senior Citizen / PWD discount (RA 11261) — does the Treasury apply this discount
    already, and if so, should CROMS display it on the assessment/slip?
16. Does the monthly collection report CROMS now produces (by fee / by source / by
    method) match the format the office actually submits?

## F. Delayed birth registration

17. Item (c) on the office's own requirement card lists eight evidences of birth and
    asks for "any two." Is that literally correct, or are some of the eight preferred
    or required over the others?
18. Does this office actually run the 10-day public posting period for a delayed birth
    registration, the way it does for a marriage licence — or does it only collect the
    checklist without a formal posting step?

## G. Workflow

19. Staff permissions — who may do what, by role?
20. Can a transaction be transferred mid-flight from one staff member/window to another?
21. Queue behaviour — anything beyond what Queue Management already does?
22. May one employee hold several roles at once?

## H. Forms — confirm fidelity (not a question, a check)

23. The blank MF-102 (2007) now on file is a third-party scan (found online), not the
    office's own stock — please confirm it matches what Peñablanca actually issues
    before it becomes the printed background on real certificates.
24. The Parental Consent (MF 06) and Parental Advice (MF 68) blanks on file are
    re-typed Word documents, not scans of the office's stock — please confirm the
    wording and layout match the office's actual sheets.
